Calculus

Study the fundamental structures of calculus.

Limits

The limit describes the behavior of a function as its variable approaches a particular value.

$$\lim_{x\to a} f(x)=L$$

Derivative

The derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change of a function.

$$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$$

Integral

Integration can represent accumulation, signed area and antiderivatives.

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$$

Real Analysis

Rigorous study of real-valued functions, sequences and convergence.

Sequences

A sequence is a function from the natural numbers to a mathematical set.

$$a_n\to L$$

Continuity

A function is continuous at a point when its limiting value agrees with its value.

$$\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$$

Uniform Convergence

Uniform convergence controls the approximation simultaneously over the entire domain.

Complex Analysis

The study of complex-valued functions.

Holomorphic Functions

A complex function is holomorphic when it is complex differentiable in a neighborhood.

$$f'(z)= \lim_{h\to0} \frac{f(z+h)-f(z)}{h}$$

Residue Theorem

Residues allow contour integrals to be evaluated from the singularities inside a contour.

$$\oint_C f(z)\,dz = 2\pi i \sum_k \operatorname{Res}(f,z_k)$$

Contour Integration

Integration can be performed along curves in the complex plane.

$$\int_C f(z)\,dz$$

Linear Algebra

Mathematical structures based on vectors, matrices and linear transformations.

Vector Spaces

A vector space is a set equipped with vector addition and scalar multiplication.

Eigenvalues

An eigenvector changes only by a scalar factor under a linear transformation.

$$A\mathbf v=\lambda\mathbf v$$

Matrix Multiplication

$$(AB)_{ij} = \sum_k A_{ik}B_{kj}$$

Classical Mechanics

The mathematical description of motion and dynamics.

Newton's Second Law

The net force determines acceleration.

$$\mathbf F=m\mathbf a$$

Lagrangian Mechanics

The dynamics can be formulated using the Lagrangian $L=T-V$.

$$L=T-V$$

Euler–Lagrange Equation

The equations of motion are obtained from the stationary action principle.

$$ \frac{d}{dt} \left( \frac{\partial L} {\partial \dot q_i} \right) - \frac{\partial L} {\partial q_i} =0 $$

Quantum Mechanics

Mathematical foundations of quantum theory.

Schrödinger Equation

$$ i\hbar \frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t} = \hat H\psi $$

Quantum Operators

Physical observables are represented by operators acting on quantum states.

$$\hat A|\psi\rangle$$

Hilbert Space

Quantum states are represented as vectors in a Hilbert space.

Spacetime Interval

$$ ds^2= -c^2dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2 $$
